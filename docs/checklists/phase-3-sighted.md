# Phase 3 checklist for a sighted helper: the map, the controls and the highlight

These steps need sight to judge. They are for a sighted or partially sighted helper, if you have one. If you don't, skip them: the automatic tests check the contrast and what is drawn, but not how it looks.

Do them on the desktop version first, then in the web version in Firefox and in Chrome or Edge. Start the desktop as in docs/checklists/phase-3-desktop.md, and the web version as in docs/checklists/phase-3-web.md. Write down each step's number and what you saw.

## 1. The status line and the move and zoom buttons (P3-T18, VIS-1)

Start at 51.06,-1.31, in Winchester.

* 1.1 Under the map is a status line with the coordinates, then the facing, the orientation, the mode, the zoom and the step. It changes after every step.
* 1.2 Under it are eight buttons: North, South, West, East, Zoom in, Zoom out, Larger step and Smaller step. Click each. Each does what its key does, and the keyboard focus stays on the map.
* 1.3 Press O for cardinal facing. The four arrow buttons are now Forward, Back, Turn left and Turn right. Press O until north-fixed.
* 1.4 Press M for hierarchy mode. The arrow buttons are now Up, Down, Previous neighbour and Next neighbour. Click Up: the same list opens as when you press the Up key. Cancel, and press M.
* 1.5 In Settings, Display, switch off "Show the status line and the move and zoom buttons under the map". Both go. Switch it on again.

## 2. The whole world on the map (P3-T1)

* 2.1 Zoom out with Page Up until you see the whole globe. Russia, Canada and the North Atlantic are each one shape, not a small square.
* 2.2 At 0 degrees north and 0 degrees east there is no small square in the ocean any more.
* 2.3 Zoom in on Switzerland. Lakes are drawn as water. Mountain ranges and deserts are not filled, as they overlap.

## 3. Regions and the outline (P3-T17, P2-T17v)

* 3.1 In Settings, Display, "Draw the borders of regions" is off. Regions such as England are not drawn, and the region you are in is still outlined. Switch it on: their borders appear. Switch it off again.
* 3.2 Start at 56.0,3.0, in the North Sea. Try each choice of "Outline the current area": always, not for the oceans, never for oceans and seas, and never. Check the North Sea's outline does what each says.
* 3.3 In the layers list, switch on "Special lines: equator, prime meridian, tropics, polar circles and date line". In each theme (T), they are thicker and in a colour of their own. Switch them off again.

## 4. The highlight of the selected item (P3-T4f)

Start at 46.8,8.2, zoom 1,000 kilometres.

* 4.1 Press Shift+5 for Bordering and move to Nidwalden. It is outlined on the map in the highlight colour.
* 4.2 Press Shift+7 for Crossing and move to the A8. The road is highlighted.
* 4.3 Press Shift+6 for Overlapping and move to Swiss Prealps. Nothing is highlighted, because mountain ranges are not drawn. This is known, and DP186 asks about it. Should it be?
* 4.4 In the high contrast themes, is the highlight easy to tell from the current area's outline?

## 5. Hierarchy mode and names on the map (P3-T6, P3-T12)

* 5.1 In hierarchy mode, go Up from Hampshire to England. The map jumps to fit England, and England is outlined.
* 5.2 In Settings, Speech, set Names to "Always the local name". At 51.0,10.0 the map's names are in German, such as Deutschland. Set it back.
