# Phase 3 tidy-up 2 checklist for a sighted helper: the map's drawing and the dialogs

These steps need sight to judge. They are for a sighted or partially sighted helper, if you have one. If you don't, skip them: the automatic tests check what is drawn, but not how it looks. They cover only what changed in the second tidy-up plan (docs/plan/phase-3-tidy-up-plan2.md): how the map draws the stand-in areas, the two M3s, the Moselle condominium and the places that are gone, and how the Map views dialog and the Settings Keys controls look.

Do them on the desktop version first, with the world pack, then in the web version in Firefox. Start the desktop as in docs/checklists/phase-3-tidy-up-2-desktop.md, and the web version as in docs/checklists/phase-3-tidy-up-2-web.md. Both start with Reset all settings from the Map menu, then Reset. Use "Go to" with Ctrl+G for each place.

Write down each step's number, such as 3.2, and what you saw. Put your notes in a text file next to this one, docs/checklists/phase-3-tidy-up-2-sighted-results.txt. Lines starting with ">" are fine for answers to questions. The places and words below were checked on the world pack of 20 November. A difference only in a number is not a fault unless it seems wrong.

In the web version, Ctrl+Alt+M is the menu bar and Ctrl+Comma is Settings.

## 1. Stand-in areas are drawn as part of the Counties layer (P3-T28a)

Go to 55.76,-6.28, on Islay, at zoom 100 kilometres.

* 1.1 Press Ctrl+Shift+L for the layers list and make sure Counties is on. Close the list.
* 1.2 Islay is inside the area "Argyll and Bute", which is outlined as the area you are in.
* 1.3 Go to 58.0012,-6.4323, at zoom 25 kilometres, on a small island off the east coast of Lewis, in the Outer Hebrides. Scotland's land that is in no council area is a few small islands in the north-west, about 8 square kilometres in all, drawn as one area, "Part of Scotland in no county". Space says "Part of Scotland in no county, Scotland, United Kingdom, Eilean Liubhaird, Outer Hebrides, British Isles, Europe". Is it drawn beside the council area round it, with no gap between them?
* 1.4 Go to 50.3625,-3.5790, at zoom 10 kilometres, by the mouth of the River Dart near Dartmouth in Devon. England's land in no county is here, about 2 square kilometres, drawn as an area named "Part of England in no county". Space says "Near Dartmouth. Part of England in no county, England, United Kingdom, Great Britain, British Isles, Europe". See whether the name appears on the screen as well as being spoken. There is none along Hampshire's coast.
* 1.5 In the layers list, switch off Counties. The stand-in areas go with the Counties layer: nothing of them is drawn. Switch it on again.
* 1.6 Do the stand-in areas look the same as the real counties, in the same colour and with the same kind of border? They should: nothing marks them as different.

## 2. The two M3s (P3-T28d, P3-T21b)

Press Ctrl+F, choose "Only Motorways and trunk roads", type m3 and press Enter on "M3, Northern Ireland, United Kingdom, road", eighth in the list. Zoom in to 10 kilometres.

* 2.1 A road is drawn in Belfast, in the colour of motorways and trunk roads. The highlight, if you press Shift+5 and move to it, is the same road.
* 2.2 Go to the English M3 by searching again and choosing "M3, England, United Kingdom, road", ninth in the list, at zoom 100 kilometres. A long line is drawn running from near London, through Surrey and Hampshire, south-west to Southampton. It is a different line from the one in Belfast: there is no line drawn between Belfast and England.
* 2.3 Zoom out to 2,500 kilometres. Is each M3 drawn, or only when zoomed in? Say which.
* 2.4 Go to Belfast, at zoom 5 kilometres. The M3 crosses the River Lagan on a bridge. The road is drawn unbroken over the river, and the river is drawn under or over it in a way you can follow. How does it look?

## 4. The Moselle condominium (P3-T28f)

Press Ctrl+F, type moselle condominium and press Enter on it. Zoom in to 10 kilometres.

* 4.1 It is drawn as a long thin area along the river, between the land of Germany and of Luxembourg.
* 4.2 Is it outlined as the area you are in, and is the outline easy to see, though it is thin?
* 4.3 In each theme (press T), does it keep its place in the drawing order, as part of the Countries layer, above the countries? It should not hide the border of Germany or Luxembourg completely.
* 4.4 Zoom out to 250 kilometres. Do you still see the condominium, or does it disappear? Say which.

## 5. The coast and the sea (P3-T28n)

Go to 54.68,-5.78, in Belfast Lough, at zoom 25 kilometres.

* 5.1 Belfast Lough and the North Channel are drawn as sea. Is the sea in one colour with no line across it between the lough and the North Channel? There should be none.
* 5.2 The outline "Sea of the Hebrides" is drawn, when you are in it, as one shape that reaches Belfast Lough. Does the outline look odd? The PO has a decision pending on this, in desktop step 16.4.

## 7. The Map views dialog (P3-T28h)

Press Ctrl+Shift+V.

* 7.1 The list shows each view's name, with the view in use marked "in use" in words. The mark is not only a colour or an icon.
* 7.2 Beside or under the list are the buttons Add, Rename and Delete. Then OK and Cancel. "Save the layers as a view" is not there. Are the buttons in a sensible order, and all fully visible, with no text cut off?
* 7.3 The focus is shown by an outline on the view in use. Press Tab through each button: is each focus easy to see?
* 7.4 Press Add. A small window asks for a name. Is it clear what to type? Press Cancel.
* 7.5 OK is drawn as the default button, in a way you can see. Press Enter with a view chosen: it is the same as pressing OK.
* 7.6 Make the window narrower, or the text larger, if the system allows it. Do the buttons stay in view?
* 7.7 Close with Cancel.

## 8. The Settings Keys controls (P3-T31b)

Press Ctrl+Comma and go to the Keys tab, in the desktop version, then in the web version.

* 8.1 At the top is a "Key map" list, with its label beside or above it. Its choices include "Windows desktop" on the desktop, or "Windows browser" in the web version, and "Alternative". The current choice is easy to see.
* 8.2 Below it are the commands with their keys, and the buttons "Save keys as", "Open key file" and "Reset keys". Are all three buttons visible without scrolling, and not cut off?
* 8.3 Choose "Alternative". The commands' keys on the screen change at once, such as Settings to Ctrl+Shift+X. Is the change easy to notice?
* 8.4 After "Save keys as", "Open key file" and "Reset keys", is the result shown as words on the screen, such as in a status message, and not only spoken? Say where it appears.
* 8.5 In the web version, "Open key file" is a file input. Does it look like the other buttons, or like a different kind of control? Is its label readable?
* 8.6 In each theme (press T), and in the high contrast themes, are the Keys controls easy to read, with focus easy to see?
* 8.7 Choose "Windows desktop" or "Windows browser" again, and press "Reset keys", then OK.

## 9. The Information panel (P3-T29)

Go to 51.06,-1.31, and press Shift+1.

* 9.1 The heading is "Within, 6 items, smallest first". The headings of Nearby, Bordering, Contains, Overlapping, Passed over and Search, Shift+2 to Shift+7, show their count and order in words, and are easy to tell apart from the items.
* 9.2 In Bordering, the heading "Coast" is shown as a group, with the seas under it. In Overlapping, the headings of the layers and of "Lines crossing its border" are groups, with their items under them. Is it clear what is a heading and what is an item?
* 9.3 In Overlapping, an item such as "Great Britain, you are inside" is not cut off at the side of the window.
* 9.4 In the web version, text in the panel is not cut off at 200 percent zoom of the page (Ctrl+Plus).
