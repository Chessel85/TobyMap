DP201. Borders as a share, and where a line crosses (checklist 6.3 and 6.5). 
"Border share, recommended: add the percentage, ""Nidwalden, north-east, shared border 69 kilometres, 28 percent"", and give the whole border's length, land and coast, in Details. Easily done from the stored lengths and the shape.
Go with recommendations for bordering and crosses.
Can we also expand an entry in the bordering list to list the points at which bordering starts and ends.  If I was in the Madrid area, and saw that Spain borders Portugal, I could expand this and see two points (which are also Alba announced) giving one point to the NW and another to the SW.
Interestingly, from Madrid, there would be four points for the border with France since Andora intersects the France/Spain border.
Crossing, recommended: one item for each line, saying how many times it crosses and where the nearest crossing is: ""A8, crosses twice, nearest 6 kilometres south-west"". Under it, one item for each crossing point with its direction and distance, so Space and Enter work on each. The points are worked out from the line and the shape when the group is filled. If that is too slow on large areas, the build stores them.
Alternative: keep ""crosses it"", and only change the direction to the nearest crossing point."
