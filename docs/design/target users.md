# target users

* Fully blind users completely dependent on screen readers and refreshable Braille display.  Also covers sonification and other non-visual feedback.
* Partially sighted users who maybe able to configure the solution to best suit what vision they do have e.g. High contrast colours, isolation of displayed features
* Sighted users who work alongside blind and partially sighted users.  Although solutions like QGIS, ESRI, Google Maps etc maybe their first choice of tool, they should still be able to get value from this solution.
* Data managers who collect and organise data on behalf of map users
* Product owner who champions the benefits of the end users.
