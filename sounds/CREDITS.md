# Sound credits

Every sound TobyMap plays, where it came from and its licence (SON-8).

* click.wav, the tick, and bump.wav, the blocked sound: made by the product owner, who owns them. Released under the same GPL-3.0 licence as the code (DP128). They are kept in git (DP111).
* meridianCrossed.wav, played when the prime meridian or the 180 degree meridian is crossed, and equatorCrossed.wav, played when the equator, a tropic or a polar circle is crossed: made by the product owner, who owns them, and released and kept in the same way.
* crossed-added.wav, ding.wav, no-data.wav, ambiguous-up.wav, line-crossing.wav, beyond-view.wav, item-direction.wav and beacon.wav: placeholder tones made by tools/make_placeholder_sounds.py. They are generated, with no source recording, and released under the same GPL-3.0 licence as the code. They are replaced by your own sounds when you send them (DP111). beacon.wav is the soft whump you chose on 8 November 2026, from tools/make_beacon_samples.py.

Only the files named in mapcore/describe/earcons.yaml are shipped with the installer and the web version. Any other file in this folder stays on your computer (DP127).
* layer-continent.wav, layer-country.wav, layer-region.wav, layer-water.wav, layer-river.wav, layer-road.wav, layer-railway.wav, layer-town.wav, layer-mountain.wav and layer-peak.wav: each base layer's placeholder tone in look around and Hear its direction (P3-T11b), made by tools/make_placeholder_sounds.py, under the GPL-3.0 licence like the code.
